# Responsible AI

SkillMap provides decision support. It does not make automatic hiring, rejection, or
employment decisions.

## Intended use

- Help a person inspect skills explicitly present in a resume.
- Compare supplied resume evidence with a supplied job description.
- Identify missing taxonomy skills for review and resume editing.
- Organize a small, authorized set of documents for human review.

## Prohibited use

- Automatic hiring, rejection, ranking, compensation, or termination decisions
- Inference or scoring of protected or sensitive personal characteristics
- Candidate surveillance, identity verification, or background screening
- Claims that a score measures intelligence, suitability, truthfulness, or future success
- Processing documents without an appropriate legal basis or authorization

## Human oversight

Review the matched and missing evidence, not only the aggregate score. Confirm parser output
and allow candidates to correct inaccurate or incomplete information. Apply the same job
criteria consistently and provide an alternative review process when needed.

## Limitations and monitoring

Taxonomy gaps, formatting, language, acronyms, and indirect experience can change results.
Before organizational use, evaluate performance across relevant roles and groups, document
acceptance criteria, review errors, and suspend use when the evidence is insufficient.
